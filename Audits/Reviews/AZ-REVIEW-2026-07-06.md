## 5. NEW findings at HEAD

**NEW-1 (observation, HIGH confidence): the full suite is not deterministically green.** 1 failure in 4 runs at
byte-identical trees (2026-07-05 ×2 green; 2026-07-06 run1 `2395/2396`, run2 green). Consequence: the single
`npm test` CI job (O2) **must** ship with a deterministic/quarantine split (unit vs live-RPC/flaky) or it will
be red-noise from day one. Identity of the flake not captured (see §4 INFERRED).

**NEW-2 (CONFIRMED, 2/2 adversarial votes — from #260): low-quorum demotion silently reroutes the EXECUTED
source, and `lowConfidence` is a dead flag.** `quote-quorum.ts:112-113` (header: "display-only") demotes a
2-responder winner beyond the 500 bps band and returns `[runnerUp]`; `api.ts:250-251/318-322` derives
`meta.best` from it; `SwapBox.tsx:595/:622` **execute** `meta.best.source` and build fallbacks from the same
meta — so on a legitimately-wide thin market (long-tail/imported tokens) the true best is excluded from
execution, fallbacks, and the Compare list. The only signal, `lowConfidence` (set api.ts:252/310/322; typed
adapters/types.ts:109), is **read by no component** and not even forwarded by `/api/v1/quote` (which forwards
`crossQuoteWarning` only). Execution *gates* (SC-04/R1/minimumOutput) are untouched → not fund-flow-unsafe,
but it is an execution-quality change contradicting the module's own header. Fix direction: render the flag
(badge like "Quote only") + keep the demoted quote visible-but-unselectable, or restrict demotion to display.
**Fund-flow-adjacent (execution routing) → Auditor should glance at the fix. Rule #9-adjacent.**

**NEW-3 (REFUTED, 2/2): "v1/swap auto-selects quote-only sources → returns on-chain-reverting FeeCollector
calldata."** True premise: the #261 `executable-sources` scoping exists only in the app path (`SwapBox.tsx:156`,
`useSwap.ts:320`) — `/api/v1/swap`'s `autoSelectSource` (route.ts:281) filters only `FEE_INCOMPATIBLE_SOURCES`.
Refuted mechanism: the deployed FC checks **only** `whitelistedRouters` + H-04 `minimumOutput` on-chain — and
the repo's own probes (W7-followup §3) show OpenOcean/Curve-NG/Sushi-RP4 routers ARE whitelisted on mainnet FC
(only RedSnwapper ❌, and that build is rejected earlier by `validateRouterAddress`). SC-04/R1 are off-chain
gates that don't apply to raw-calldata API consumers. **Kept as a LOW note:** the app/API scoping asymmetry is
real and worth aligning when v1 goes multi-chain (S8), but no gas-wasting revert path was demonstrated.
